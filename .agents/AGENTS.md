# Hidden Logic Channel Learnings

## Primary Objective
Create highly relatable "hidden explanation" Shorts that make viewers realize everyday frustrations have an underlying system or manipulation. 

## Prioritized Topics
The highest-performing videos are everyday frustrations with a hidden explanation.
* Traffic jams
* Airport annoyances
* Grocery store manipulation
* Hotel design tricks
* Shopping psychology
* Queue systems
* Pricing psychology

## Strong Topic Examples
* Why grocery stores move things around
* Why airport gates keep changing
* Why traffic jams appear from nowhere
* Why restaurants use giant menus
* Why hotel hallways feel endless
* Why checkout lines always seem unfair
* Why supermarkets put essentials at the back

## Topics to Avoid
* Abstract psychology
* Niche design facts
* Educational trivia
* Topics without strong daily relevance

## Script Tone and Hook guidelines
* The viewer must experience the problem frequently.
* The title should imply a hidden reason ("Why this annoying thing happens", "This isn't an accident", "The hidden reason behind this everyday experience").
* The explanation reveals a system, incentive, or manipulation.
* The topic should create an emotional reaction such as annoyance, curiosity, suspicion, or relief.
